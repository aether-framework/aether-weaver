## 🤝 How to get on this list

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), open a pull request, and get it merged. Every kind of
contribution counts — code, tests, documentation, a reproducer on an issue that turns a vague
report into a fixable one.

Contributions are made under the [Developer Certificate of Origin](DCO); see
[AI_USAGE.md](AI_USAGE.md) for how AI-assisted work is handled.
